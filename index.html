About

Knott Advisory is led by Andrew Knott — a Chartered Engineer and Chartered Manager (CMgr FCMI) with over three decades of senior leadership in pharmaceutical and medical device automation, validation, and MES. Andrew served as a Business Unit Director for twelve years and has operated his own consultancy for thirteen years, directing complex Industry 4.0 programmes across Europe.

He combines practitioner depth with academic distinction — an MBA (Distinction) from Leeds University Business School and current doctoral research in Industry 4.0/5.0 at the University of Strathclyde, Department of Design, Manufacturing & Engineering Management (DMEM). This unique blend enables independent, evidence-led guidance at board and executive levels — translating strategy into outcomes without hype.
